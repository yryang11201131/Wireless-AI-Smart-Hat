\# DoA Results



The Direction of Arrival experiment evaluated sound-source direction estimation using the ReSpeaker USB microphone array.



The microphone array can provide angular direction information around the user.



For the final smart-hat implementation, the direction output is simplified into three major directions:



\- Front

\- Left

\- Right



This simplified representation is used to improve readability and provide intuitive LED direction feedback.



Experimental diagrams and direction-test records can be placed in this directory.
