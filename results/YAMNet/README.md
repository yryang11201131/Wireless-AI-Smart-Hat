\# YAMNet Results



The environmental sound recognition experiment evaluated four sound categories:



\- Alarm

\- Car Horn

\- Dog Bark

\- Background



After data augmentation, approximately 4,000 test samples were used for evaluation.



The observed classification accuracies were:



| Category | Accuracy |

|---|---:|

| Alarm | 90.0% |

| Car Horn | 89.4% |

| Dog Bark | 93.1% |

| Background | 98.4% |



The experiment was used to evaluate the feasibility of YAMNet-based environmental sound recognition for the smart hat.



Additional result files such as confusion matrices and evaluation summaries can be stored in this directory.
